How to Setup IPTV on Samsung Tizen & LG webOS Smart TVs

📅 Published: September 12, 2026 ✍️ Author: GO GO IPTV ⏱️ 5 Min Read

Why Choose GO GO IPTV for Your Smart TV?

GO GO IPTV offers a robust library of live channels, VOD titles, and an intuitive interface that runs natively on both Samsung Tizen and LG webOS platforms. Because the service is delivered via an app rather than a hardware box, you keep your living‑room clean while enjoying high‑definition streams.

Prerequisites Before You Begin

  • Active GO GO IPTV subscription (username & password).
  • Stable internet connection – at least 10 Mbps for HD, 25 Mbps for 4K.
  • Samsung TV (2016 or newer) running Tizen OS or LG TV (2015 or newer) running webOS.
  • Remote control with a working Enter/OK button.

Step‑by‑Step Setup on Samsung Tizen

  1. Turn on your TV and press the Home button on the remote.
  2. Select Apps from the bottom menu.
  3. In the Apps screen, choose the Search icon (magnifying glass) and type GO GO IPTV.
  4. If the app appears, select Install. If it does not appear, you need to enable Unknown Sources:
    1. Go to Settings > General > External Device Manager > Device Connect Manager.
    2. Turn Allow Apps from Unknown Sources on.
    3. Return to the Apps store and repeat the search.
  5. After installation, open the app. You will see a login screen.
  6. Enter your GO GO IPTV username and password, then press Login.
  7. Navigate the channel guide, adjust video quality in Settings > Playback if needed, and start watching.

Step‑by‑Step Setup on LG webOS

  1. Press the Home button on your LG remote.
  2. Select LG Content Store from the launcher bar.
  3. Choose the Search tab (magnifying glass) and type GO GO IPTV.
  4. If the app is listed, click Install. If not, enable Developer Mode:
    1. Open Settings > General > About This TV > Developer Mode.
    2. Toggle Developer Mode on and set a password (any 4‑digit code).
    3. Return to the LG Content Store and repeat the search.
  5. Launch the newly installed GO GO IPTV app.
  6. Enter your subscription credentials and confirm.
  7. Use the remote’s directional pad to browse categories, then press OK to start a stream.

Common Configuration Tweaks

Both platforms let you fine‑tune playback to match your network:

  • Auto‑adjust bitrate: Enable this to let the app switch between 720p, 1080p, or 4K based on real‑time bandwidth.
  • Subtitle settings: Go to Settings > Subtitles and choose the default language or turn them off.
  • Picture mode: For the best color accuracy, set your TV to Standard or Cinema while streaming.

Troubleshooting Guide

1. App Does Not Appear in the Store

Make sure your TV firmware is up to date. On Samsung, go to Settings > Support > Software Update. On LG, navigate to Settings > All Settings > General > About This TV > Check for Updates. After updating, repeat the search.

2. Login Fails – “Invalid credentials”

Double‑check the username and password on the GO GO IPTV website. If you recently changed the password, log out of the app (Settings > Account > Logout) and sign in again.

3. Buffering or Stuttering

  1. Test your internet speed with a speed‑test app on a phone. If it’s below the recommended threshold, reboot your router.
  2. In the GO GO IPTV app, disable Auto‑adjust bitrate and manually select a lower resolution (e.g., 720p).
  3. Close other streaming devices that might be consuming bandwidth.

4. No Sound

Check the TV’s audio output settings. On Samsung, go to Settings > Sound > Sound Output and select TV Speaker. On LG, choose Settings > Sound > Sound Out and ensure it’s not set to an external device you’re not using.

5. App Crashes Immediately

Clear the app cache (Settings > Apps > GO GO IPTV > Clear Cache) and restart the TV. If the problem persists, uninstall and reinstall the app.

Best Practices for a Seamless Experience

  • Keep your TV’s firmware and the GO GO IPTV app updated – updates often include performance fixes.
  • Use a wired Ethernet connection if possible; Wi‑Fi can introduce latency on larger streams.
  • Create a shortcut on the home screen for quick access: press and hold the app icon, then select Add to Home.

FAQ

Below are the most common questions users ask after installing GO GO IPTV on Samsung or LG smart TVs.

  • Can I use GO GO IPTV on older Samsung models that run older Tizen versions? Yes, but the app may require at least Tizen 3.0. Check your TV’s software version under Settings > Support > About This TV.
  • Do I need a separate IPTV box? No. The GO GO IPTV app runs directly on the TV, eliminating the need for extra hardware.
  • Is there a way to record live TV? The GO GO IPTV app does not include a native DVR function, but you can use an external USB recorder that supports HDMI input.
  • How many devices can I stream simultaneously? Your subscription allows up to three concurrent streams. Exceeding this limit will result in a “Maximum devices reached” error.
  • What if my TV’s app store is blocked in my country? You can sideload the GO GO IPTV APK using a USB drive and the TV’s developer mode, but be sure to follow the manufacturer’s guidelines.

With these steps, you should be able to enjoy GO GO IPTV on both Samsung Tizen and LG webOS smart TVs without hassle. Happy streaming!
